This reference defines the APIs and data fields **VentureOne requires from ADREC** (Abu Dhabi Real Estate Centre) to onboard an issuer, verify a property, transfer title into an SPV, and keep a live tokenized offering in sync with the registry.

It is written **from VentureOne's side of the integration**: every endpoint, field, and encryption rule below is a request *to* ADREC, describing what we need ADREC to expose. Paths, authentication, and certificate details are proposals pending ADREC's contract.

<Info>
  This document supersedes the earlier property-only reference. It reconciles that draft with VentureOne's full API checklist and the platform's own data model. See [Scope & reconciliation](/introduction/scope-and-reconciliation) for what changed, what was consolidated, and the open gaps.
</Info>

## What VentureOne is building

VentureOne tokenizes Abu Dhabi real estate. An issuer's property is verified against the ADREC registry, legal title is moved into a special-purpose vehicle (SPV), the deed is annotated as tokenized, and investors subscribe to fractional tokens. Throughout the offering's life, VentureOne must detect any registry change that affects the asset.

ADREC is the **system of record** for: property attributes, ownership, encumbrances, title documents, valuations, licensed parties (property managers, brokers, valuers, developers), tenancy (Tawtheeq), and the beneficial-ownership register.

## End-to-end flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Onboard the issuer">
    Verify the issuer's registration ID / trade licence against ADREC, then resolve the properties they own.
    <br />→ [Party Verification](/endpoints/party-verification) · [Owner Properties](/endpoints/owner-properties)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the property">
    Pull the canonical property record — attributes, ownership, encumbrances, zoning, eligibility — and the supporting documents and valuation.
    <br />→ [Property Details](/endpoints/property-details) · [Documents](/endpoints/documents) · [Valuation](/endpoints/valuation) · [Ownership History](/endpoints/ownership-history)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm & freeze the title state">
    Re-check freshness at draft time and capture an authoritative, signed title snapshot as compliance evidence.
    <br />→ [Property Status](/endpoints/property-status) · [Title State](/endpoints/title-state)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move title & annotate">
    Quote registry fees, transfer title issuer → SPV, register any charge in the SPV's favour, and annotate the deed as tokenized. Pay the fee to finalise.
    <br />→ [Fee Quote](/endpoints/fee-quote) · [Registry Transactions](/endpoints/registry-transactions) · [Fee Payment](/endpoints/fee-payment)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report beneficial owners">
    As investors subscribe and trade, keep ADREC's beneficial-ownership register in sync with the cap table.
    <br />→ [Beneficial Owners](/endpoints/beneficial-owners)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Operate & monitor">
    Track tenancy and revenue inputs, and receive webhooks for any title, ownership, encumbrance, or tenancy change so live offerings can be frozen.
    <br />→ [Tenancy](/endpoints/tenancy) · [Rental Index](/endpoints/rental-index) · [Change Webhooks](/endpoints/change-webhooks)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Requirement levels

Every field in this reference carries one of these markers:

| Marker | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **`REQUIRED`** | Blocker — VentureOne cannot proceed without it |
| **`OPTIONAL`** | Enrichment — improves the listing or model, not a gate |
| **`CONDITIONAL`** | Case-specific — required only in a named scenario (e.g. units, off-plan) |
| **`ISSUER`** | Supplied on VentureOne, **not** by ADREC — listed only to show the full picture |
