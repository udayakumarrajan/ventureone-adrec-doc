---
title: "Approve or reject"
description: "An ADREC officer decides a pending request, with a reason."
---

```http
POST /approvals/{approvalId}/approve
POST /approvals/{approvalId}/reject
```

**Officer token only.** VentureOne cannot call these. The decision and its reason are written to ADREC's [audit trail](/endpoints/approvals-audit) with the officer's name.

## Body

| Field | Type | Req. | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `reason` | string | Recommended | Up to 500 characters. Shown to VentureOne's operators, and required in practice on a rejection: it is what they act on |

## Rules

- Only a `PENDING` request can be decided. A second decision is `1409`.
- An expired request cannot be approved (`1000`); VentureOne files it again.
- **A rejection is final for that request.** VentureOne holds the raise: nothing is minted and nothing is re-sent automatically. An operator either files a **new** request, linked to the rejected one, or cancels the raise and refunds its investors.

## Response

The [approval request](/endpoints/approvals-read#the-approval-request) with `state` `APPROVED` or `REJECTED`, `decidedBy`, `decidedAt` and `decisionReason`.
